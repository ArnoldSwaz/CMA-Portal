Portal Guidance, Accessibility & Data Protection Statement

Regulatory reference guide for applicants, licensed market intermediaries, and supervisory officers.

1. Accessibility Statement (WCAG 2.1 Level AA)

The CMA Online Regulatory & Licensing Portal is engineered to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards:

  • Minimum 4.5:1 text contrast across CMA Navy (#0F3A5F) and Regulatory Green (#177A43) tokens.
  • Dual-coded status indicators combining explicit text labels with semantic colour badges.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible amber focus rings (:focus-visible) and prefers-reduced-motion support.
  • Dedicated print stylesheets for official certificates and supervisory compliance reports.

2. Data Protection & Audit Integrity

Personal and corporate records submitted to CMA-ORLP are governed by the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya) and the Capital Markets Act (Cap. 485A):

  • Every state mutation is recorded in an immutable append-only audit log (aud.audit_event) attributed to the acting app.account_id.
  • Sensitive Key Person Fit & Proper disclosures are restricted to authorized CMA Licensing & Legal officers via role-based access control.
  • Public Register searches expose only statutory licence metadata and verification codes.
